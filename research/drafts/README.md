R4 carve plans (`<cycle>-<theme>.yaml`): the nodes, edges and quality edges one
cycle proposes, with their evidence, contested triggers, debate ids, verification
verdicts and mint status. Written by the ontologist and edge drafter (Stage 3C);
read by every later R4 step and by `coverage report.py dossier` (Stage 3F).
A carve plan is a proposal — only `status: minted` entries exist in the store.
An entry marked `blocked: structure` is a carve the seed structure cannot hold (D70); it is
never debated, verified or minted, and is linked to a `structure-friction` finding the
structure review reads (`langatlas-research structure report`).
